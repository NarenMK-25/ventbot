VentBot

The cubicle confessional & jargon exorcist.

ABOUT THIS APP

Two modes. One goal: make the workday lighter.

VentBot reads whatever you paste in and figures out whether it's corporate jargon that needs decoding, or a rough day that needs a witness. No forms, no categories to pick — just say what's going on.

🧠 Jargon mode

Drop in the email, the Slack message, the meeting recap — anything dense with "synergy" and "circling back." You get the real meaning, a five-year-old's version, and a pirate translation, side by side.

😮‍💨 Vent mode

Tell it how the day actually went. VentBot responds with a bit of empathy and, sometimes, a small joke to take the edge off — no fixing, no advice, just a place to put it down.

GOOD TO KNOW

Sessions are anonymous and kept only in your browser. VentBot isn't a therapist — if a vent reads like it's more than a bad day, it will gently point you toward real support instead of trying to handle it alone.

VentBot — built for cubicle survivors. Not a substitute for professional support.
